Reporting Methodology
High Risk: Assessments with a fatigue score greater than 12.

Morning Assessments: 0700 through 0959.

Overnight Assessments: 2200 through 0559. Assessments completed after midnight are assigned to the preceding operational night.

Time Zone: Central Time, including daylight saving adjustments.

Privacy: Reports contain aggregate statistics and exclude provider names.
